AI replies and privacy

Bottle uses Groq to generate AI replies. Groq can receive your request, relevant conversation context and text from supported attachments. A fallback provider can receive the same information if one is configured. In a server, others with access to the channel or thread may see your request and Bottle’s reply.

AI replies can be inaccurate, incomplete or inappropriate. Check important information independently and seek qualified help for medical, legal, financial or safety-critical decisions.

Use /forget to clear Bottle’s chat memory for you in the current channel or thread. That command leaves Discord messages in place. AI usage limits

Bottle’s default limits apply to successful AI replies in any rolling 12-hour period.

Per user
Up to 30 replies across all servers
Shared
Up to 150 replies across all users and servers

Both limits apply. A counted reply stops using a slot 12 hours after it was counted. Use /quota to check your balance, the shared balance and when your oldest counted reply expires. Utility commands such as /quota, /help, /staff and /forget use no AI quota.

Cooldowns, safety checks and Groq’s capacity limits can restrict AI replies before either limit is reached. Bottle reserves 20% of its configured provider capacity by default, so the limits are maximums. Bottle may suppress repeated limit warnings.

Moderation

Bottle may decline requests or withhold AI replies under its safety rules. Authorized server staff can also enable optional Discord AutoMod rules through Bottle. Enabled rules can block matching messages and may send alerts to a channel designated by the server’s staff. Contact the server’s moderation team about a server rule or a moderation decision.

Discord runs enabled native AutoMod rules, so they can remain active while Bottle is offline. Server staff can review and disable Bottle-managed rules through /automod.
