## 1. Eligibility and server permissions

You must be at least 13, meet Discord’s minimum age for your country, and have any parent or guardian permission Discord requires. Follow the [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms), the [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), applicable law, and the rules of the server where you use Bottle. Install or configure Bottle only for a server you are authorized to manage.

## 2. Acceptable use

Use Bottle only for lawful purposes. Submit prompts or attachments only if you have permission to share them. Keep passwords, API keys, recovery codes, and private information out of your requests.

- Do not use Bottle to harass, threaten, impersonate, scam, or expose someone’s private information.
- Do not request hateful, sexually explicit, exploitative, or unlawful content, or use Bottle to infringe intellectual property or other rights.
- Do not spam commands, evade usage limits, bypass safety checks, or interfere with the bot or Discord.

Bottle may refuse requests or withhold replies. The operator may restrict or suspend access when necessary to address abuse, protect users, or comply with platform rules or applicable law.

## 3. AI replies, channel visibility, and privacy

Bottle generates replies using an AI provider. Prompts, relevant recent chat context, and supported attachment text may be sent to the provider to generate or review an answer. In a server channel, people who can access the channel may see your request and Bottle’s reply. See the [Privacy Policy](https://mischyef.github.io/bottle-terms/privacy.html) for details about processing, storage, and retention.

AI replies can be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. Check important information independently before relying on it. Bottle does not provide professional medical, legal, or financial advice and should not be used as an emergency service.

Use `/forget` to clear Bottle’s local chat memory for your conversation in the current channel or thread. It does not delete Discord messages or data already handled by an AI provider. For personal data access or deletion, see [Contact & Data Requests](https://mischyef.github.io/bottle-terms/privacy.html#contact-data-requests).

## 4. Usage limits and availability

Bottle currently allows up to **30 successful AI replies per user** and **150 successful AI replies shared across servers** in a rolling 12-hour window. Both limits apply together. A reply stops counting 12 hours after it was delivered. The `/quota` command shows the current balances and when the oldest counted personal reply expires.

Cooldowns, safety checks, or provider budgets can pause AI replies sooner. Repeated requests after a limit is reached may receive no reply. Utility commands such as `/quota`, `/staff`, `/help`, and `/forget` do not use AI quota. Availability can also be affected by maintenance, outages, or Discord permissions.

## 5. Server utilities and optional moderation

The `/staff` command displays members with the configured staff role without notifying them. Authorized server staff can choose to enable Discord’s native AutoMod rules through Bottle. Enabled rules may block matching messages or send alerts to a designated channel. Discord runs those rules, so they can remain active while Bottle is offline. Server staff should review or disable any rules they no longer want to use.

Contact the server’s moderation team about its rules or enforcement decisions. Use Discord’s reporting tools for issues involving Discord’s platform rules.

## 6. Stopping use and changes to these terms

You can stop using Bottle at any time. Authorized server administrators can remove it from their server through Discord. Features, limits, and availability may change. If these terms change, the operator will publish the revised terms and effective date on this page. These terms do not limit rights or remedies that applicable law gives you.
